# PRODUCT.md — Labyrinth

## What this is

A single-page immersive brand site for a physical puzzle: a bronze labyrinth
cylinder (two interlocking halves, a maze carved across the surface, the path
itself is the lock). The site IS the experience: you enter a torch-lit stone
corridor, walk toward the artifact, touch it, and descend into the story of
the object.

## Users

Design-literate visitors arriving from awards galleries, social links, and
portfolio shares. They are on desktop with good GPUs more often than average,
but mobile must not break. They give the page 10 seconds to earn attention.

## Brand

- Register: **brand** — the design is the product.
- World: subterranean, lithic, warm-lit. Bronze, stone, moss, torchlight.
- Voice: terse, assured, slightly mythic. Never explains twice. No exclamation
  marks. No marketing superlatives ("revolutionary", "stunning").
- The object is treated as an artifact, not a SKU.

## Anti-references

- Medieval-fantasy kitsch (parchment textures, blackletter type, dragon clipart).
- Game UI (health bars, WASD prompts, minimaps). This is a site, not a game.
- Generic SaaS landing patterns: hero-metric blocks, identical feature card
  grids, gradient text, glassmorphism panels.
- Haunted-house horror. The mood is reverent and quiet, not scary.

## Strategic principles

1. One continuous descent: every section is a deeper chamber, the scroll is
   the journey. Nothing should feel like a separate "page".
2. The 3D corridor is the opening act, not the whole show. Typography and
   pacing carry the second half.
3. Restraint over spectacle in the content sections: the corridor already
   spent the spectacle budget.
4. Everything readable without the WebGL canvas (progressive enhancement,
   reduced-motion, slow GPU).
